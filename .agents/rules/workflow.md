# Workflow Rules

## Spec-Driven Development
- Write design specs in `docs/specs/`.
- Wait for user approval before implementing.
- **Archive on Completion**: Move completed specs to `docs/specs/archive/`.

## TODO Management
- Use `- [ ]` and `- [x]` in `TODO.md`.
- Keep it organized by "Current Sprint" and "Backlog".

## Changelog Management
- Follow the "Keep a Changelog" format.
- Document all user-facing changes, bug fixes, and deprecations.
